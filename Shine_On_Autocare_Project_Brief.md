## Overview

This document outlines the proposed website redesign, SEO strategy, Google Ads setup, and ongoing digital marketing plan for Shine On Autocare. The goal is to increase online visibility, drive more qualified traffic to the website, improve conversions (phone calls, quote requests), and establish a strong digital presence in both the Pleasanton and San Antonio markets.

### TL;DR (Quick Wins Summary)

1. Full Wix website redesign with mobile-first, conversion-optimized layout
2. Dedicated landing pages for every service (required for Google Ads)
3. Complete SEO foundation: meta tags, structured data, heading hierarchy, keyword targeting
4. Google Ads campaign setup with proper conversion tracking
5. Ongoing SEO and content strategy to build organic traffic over time

## Current Website Audit

### What's Working
- The Wix site is live and functional
- Business info (address, email, service area) is present
- Some SEO basics are in place
- Good service imagery and video content on the homepage

### What Needs Improvement

| Issue | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| No meta titles or descriptions rendering properly | Google can't display your pages well in search results | High |
| No structured data (JSON-LD schema) | Missing from Google's local business features, Knowledge Panel, rich snippets | High |
| No Open Graph or Twitter Card tags | Links shared on social media have no image, title, or description | Medium |
| No Google Analytics or Tag Manager detected | No way to track traffic, behavior, or conversions | High |
| Only 3 pages indexed by Google (Home, Services, Contact) | Extremely thin site footprint, hurting search rankings | High |
| No dedicated landing pages for individual services | Google Ads were shut down because of this | Critical |
| No canonical URLs set | Risk of duplicate content issues | Medium |
| No blog or content strategy | Missing out on organic traffic from informational searches | Medium |

### Google Ads Issue
The previous Google Ads campaign was shut down because there were no dedicated landing pages for services. Google requires that ad traffic lands on a page relevant to the ad content, with clear conversion actions. Sending all traffic to the homepage doesn't give Google enough data to track conversions or optimize ad spend.

### Phase 2: SEO Foundation

**Goal:** Make the site visible to Google and rank for local detailing searches in both Pleasanton and San Antonio.

**What's Included:**

#### Meta Tags & On-Page SEO
- Unique `<title>` and `<meta description>` for every page via Wix SEO settings
- Keyword targeting for both "Pleasanton TX" and "San Antonio" markets
- Proper heading hierarchy (single H1 per page, structured H2s and H3s)
- Image alt text on all images for accessibility and SEO
- Canonical URLs configured in Wix

#### Structured Data (JSON-LD Schema)
- `LocalBusiness` / `AutoRepair` schema with address, hours, service area, services
- `Service` schema on each service landing page
- `areaServed` targeting both Pleasanton and San Antonio
- Implemented via Wix's custom code or structured data apps
- This helps Google display rich results, Knowledge Panel info, and local pack listings

#### Open Graph & Social Sharing
- Full OG tags (title, description, image, URL) on every page via Wix SEO settings
- Twitter Card meta tags for social sharing
- When someone shares a link to the site, it will display a proper preview with image and description

#### Technical SEO
- Clean URL structure configured in Wix
- Fast page loads optimized through Wix's built-in performance tools
- Proper sitemap (auto-generated by Wix) and robots.txt
- Mobile-responsive across all devices
- SSL enabled (included with Wix)

### Phase 3: Google Ads Setup

**Goal:** Get Google Ads running again with proper landing pages and conversion tracking.

**What's Included:**

- **Campaign structure:** Separate campaigns for Pleasanton and San Antonio service areas
- **Ad groups** organized by service type (ceramic coating, interior detail, exterior detail, etc.)
- **Landing pages:** Each ad group points to its dedicated service page with a clear CTA
- **Conversion tracking:** Set up Google Tag Manager and conversion events for:
  - Form submissions (quote requests)
  - Phone call clicks
  - Button clicks on key CTAs
- **Ad copy:** Written to match the landing page content and target local keywords
- **Budget recommendations:** Based on the local market and competition

This solves the exact issue that caused the previous campaign to be shut down. Each service now has its own page with a clear conversion action that Google can track.

### Phase 4: Ongoing SEO & Marketing (Monthly Retainer)

**Goal:** Continuously improve search rankings, drive organic traffic, and manage Google Ads.

**Monthly activities include:**

- **SEO monitoring:** Track keyword rankings, organic traffic, and search console performance
- **Content creation:** 1-2 blog posts per month targeting long-tail keywords
  - Example topics: "How to Maintain Your Ceramic Coating," "Interior Detailing Tips for Pet Owners," "Why Mobile Detailing Saves You Time"
- **Google Ads management:** Monitor ad performance, adjust bids, update ad copy, A/B test landing pages
- **Google Business Profile optimization:** Keep the listing updated, respond to reviews, add posts
- **Monthly reporting:** Clear report showing traffic, rankings, leads, and ad performance

#### Blog Strategy (Why It Matters)
Blogs are a proven way to capture organic search traffic from people who are searching for information, not just services. These readers become aware of your brand and often convert later.

Example: Someone in San Antonio searches "how long does ceramic coating last." Your blog post answers their question, establishes your expertise, and includes a CTA to get a quote. That's a warm lead you didn't pay for.

With Wix's built-in blog, publishing new posts is straightforward — you can even draft posts yourself and have me review/optimize them before publishing.
